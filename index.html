IN THE WOODLAND RETREAT

Make yourself
comfortable.

The scene fills the screen. The little menu holds the extra controls.

On a computer

Drag empty space or scroll a trackpad to look. W A S D walks; Q / E turns. Space jumps and C crouches. You can still look while sitting or lying down.

Mouse look follows the pointer where the browser allows it. If unavailable, drag empty space or use Q/E. Escape releases mouse look. Hold an object to drag it; scrolling while holding changes its distance.

On a phone

Use the left joystick to walk. Drag the scene with your other finger to look. Hold and drag a book, cup or blanket corner, then release. Landscape gives you more room.

The phone layout was checked in a resized desktop browser. A real phone test is still needed; that is a different check.

With a VR headset

Open this site’s HTTPS link in a compatible headset browser. Enter VR appears in the menu when immersive WebXR is available.

Hold the trigger over an object to grab, then release. Hold over clear ground and release on the ring to move. Small controls below your view operate the lights, return you home and exit VR.

Headset testing is pending. This is implemented support, not a verified claim that every headset works. Camera-driven jumping and resting are disabled in VR; your own head controls the view.

The good parts.
The rough edges.

These are judgments from a reviewer who knew which model made each room. Later rooms also had more instructions, feedback, assets and time. This is not a blind or controlled test.

Progress, with real limits.

Walking, grabbing, a hinged book and flexible pages are a substantial step. Contact quality, polish and real-device verification still fall short of AA/AAA expectations.

The retreat’s tougher rubric

Visual quality17/25

Scanned furniture and a complete interior help. The landscape, small props and lighting still lack the consistency of a finished game.

Controls & interactions20/25

Seated drag and scroll, joystick movement, lamps, grabbing and page turning were exercised in the browser. Actual multitouch and headset handling remain unverified.

Physical behaviour10/20

Objects have weight and collision; the throw and pages bend. Paper contacts and fabric friction are approximations. There is no full fabric-to-object force exchange.

Performance & stability9/15

Contact calculations were optimized after a slowdown. Short desktop samples are not a sustained phone or headset performance test.

Beginner experience & delivery10/15

One collection and short tutorials help. Small objects need precision; headset testing and independent visitor access still need verification.

AA and AAA are production categories, not standardized quality certificates. Here, 90+ would require exceptional visual consistency, reliable interactions and sustained performance on the promised devices. Unknown results do not receive full credit. This build has not met that bar.

WHAT CHANGED BETWEEN THE ATTEMPTS?

The settings are only
part of the story.

Different model

Spark and Astra are different AI models. Both are AI, just as free chatbots are AI too.

Different effort

Light requests lighter reasoning. Ultra requests deeper work and can use AI helpers. More effort gives opportunities to improve; it does not guarantee a good result.

Fast is separate

Fast is a processing-speed option. It doesn’t mean a large project finishes immediately or turns a sketch into a finished game.

The first two started with the same decorating request. The later cabins had a richer brief, repeated corrections and imported models and textures. We cannot isolate which setting caused each difference. Models & effort · Fast mode

A little experimenting, at your own pace.

Credits, repairs & honest limits

Room pictures are captures of the actual demos. Scanned furniture, forest assets and material textures in the retreat come from Poly Haven under CC0. The floral paper and motorcycle print were AI-generated. Geometry, interactions and the guide were developed with AI assistance.

The retreat uses Rapier for rigid objects and a small cloth/page solver based on XPBD. It is not a production cloth system. Water is animated scenery. Furniture is not all movable. There is no audio track.

Detailed mode contains genuine 4K oak and sofa textures; other assets have lower resolution. Neither “4K” nor more polygons guarantees photorealism. There is no 8K or AAA claim.

All scores are approximate, non-blind judgments, including the guide’s self-review. No physical phone or headset has been verified in this review. Spark’s preserved original includes the startup repair needed to view it; later control repairs are only in the playable copy.
