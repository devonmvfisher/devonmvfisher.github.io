Click the sky to fly; Esc returns the cursor. Drag still looks. Scroll thrusts; pinch zooms. A standard pad moves with its left stick and looks with its right. Triggers lift, bumpers roll, A lands or talks, B closes, X boosts, Y changes camera, D-pad changes gear or Travel, Start opens Help.

First principles: planet orbits keep real AU ratios. Some far TNOs and probes sit closer than their almanac a — the look chip says display and a. Radii are display-boosted (not true angular size) so skim works on a phone. Hyp bodies stay labeled fiction. Sol button hides Hyp. README · SCIENCE.md.
